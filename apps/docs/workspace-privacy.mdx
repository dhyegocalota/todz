---
title: Workspace privacy
description: Hide your other workspaces behind Touch ID or your Mac password, and see where they stay out of sight.
---

Workspace privacy keeps your other workspaces out of sight on your Mac. While it is on, a locked session shows only the workspaces that were selected when it locked, and draws exactly what an account holding just those workspaces draws: no placeholder, count, lock icon or name of any other workspace. Touch ID or your Mac password brings the others back.

## Turn it on

Open todz › Settings › Privacy & updates and turn on **Require Touch ID to switch workspaces**. It is off by default, and turning it on asks for nothing. **Ask again after** sets how long an unlocked session lasts: 1, 5, 15 or 60 minutes.

## Where other workspaces stay hidden

While the session is locked, every workspace outside the set that stayed visible is absent from:

- The sidebar, the workspace switcher and the workspace menu.
- The menu bar count, ribbon and popover.
- The widgets. Their cached data is purged when the session locks, so a widget never draws a hidden workspace.
- Siri and Shortcuts.
- `todz://` links, which answer as for a project that does not exist.
- Settings and its **Connected apps**.
- The morning glance, the day close and the global workspace shortcuts.

## Bring them back

With the lock on and the session unlocked, a plain click on a workspace switches to it alone, and ⌘-click or **Show Alongside** adds it to the view or takes it out, so you can show one, several or all. When the session locks, the set you had selected at that moment stays visible, even when it holds several workspaces, and **All Workspaces** is pinned to the workspaces it held, so a workspace you add later never joins. A launch opens the last selected set and never more.

Switching within that set, or taking a workspace out of it, never asks. Reaching outside it asks for Touch ID or your Mac password: choose **Switch Workspace…** (⌘K, also in the **…** menu of the menu bar popover), or add, switch to or select **All Workspaces** toward a workspace outside the set. If you refuse the prompt, nothing changes. Creating or deleting a workspace, turning the setting off and changing the time ask the same way. With the lock off, workspaces work as always.

## When it locks again

- When the Mac sleeps or the screen locks, and when the screensaver starts.
- When you quit todz: every launch starts locked.
- After the time you pick under **Ask again after**.
- When you choose **Lock Now**, which shows only while the session is unlocked.

## What it does not protect

<Warning>
  Workspace privacy is a privacy screen on your Mac, not encryption. Anyone who knows your Mac password can unlock it, and it does not change the server: an agent connected with OAuth still reaches its project.
</Warning>
