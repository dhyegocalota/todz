## 1. Get the app

todz for Mac is coming soon: join the waitlist on [todz.app](https://todz.app). Until then, build it from source as in [the self-host guide's build step](/self-host#7-build-the-macos-app-and-point-it-at-your-server), which keeps the todz cloud as its server.

## 2. Sign in

Type your email, press **Send Link** and open the link from the mail on the same Mac that asked for it.

<Note>
  Running your own server? Change it under **Server** in Settings, or from the server line of the sign-in screen. **Use todz cloud** goes back to the default. See [Self-host todz](/self-host).
</Note>
