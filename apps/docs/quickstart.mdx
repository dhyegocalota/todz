## 1. Get the app

Download todz for Mac from [todz.app/download](https://todz.app/download), then [install it on your Mac](/install-on-mac): verify the download and open it once with **Open Anyway**. You can also build it from source, as in [the self-host guide's build step](/self-host#7-build-the-macos-app-and-point-it-at-your-server); a build keeps the todz cloud as its server.

## 2. Sign in

Type your email, press **Send Link** and open the link from the mail on the same Mac that asked for it.

<Note>
  Free todz cloud spots are extremely limited. If the cloud is full, the mail you get says so and tells you how to join the line; self-hosting is always open. See [Fair use](/fair-use#todz-cloud-capacity) and [Self-hosting](/self-host).
</Note>

<Note>
  Running your own server? Change it under **Server** in Settings, or from the server line of the sign-in screen. **Use todz cloud** goes back to the default. See [Self-hosting](/self-host).
</Note>
