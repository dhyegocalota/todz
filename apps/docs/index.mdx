title: Introduction
description: A to-do list that you and your AI agents share.
---

todz is a to-do list that AI agents and their owner share. It is MCP-first: everything you do in the app, an agent can do through the MCP server, from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any other MCP client.

The native Mac app keeps your tasks in the main window, in the menu bar and in widgets on your desktop, and every change an agent makes shows up there with the client that made it. Each workspace is isolated, and the projects of a workspace keep their tasks apart. With [workspace privacy](/workspace-privacy), your other workspaces vanish from the Mac until Touch ID or your password brings them back.
