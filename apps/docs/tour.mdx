A look at the parts of todz for Mac beyond the main window. Everything here an agent can also do through [the MCP tools](/mcp-tools).

## Widgets

Widgets show Today on your desktop and tick a task in place.

![Next up: the next task and what follows it](/images/widgets/02-next-up.png)

![A large Today list](/images/widgets/04-large-today.png)

With day temperature on, overdue rows are washed faintly red, deeper the older they are.

![A medium widget with overdue rows washed faintly red](/images/day-temperature/01-overdue-widget.png)

## Menu bar

The popover leads with what is next: a ring and a count of what is left, one Up next card and a quiet queue under it, with the tasks done today folded into one line.

![The popover on a light menu bar: the ring, the Up next card and the queue](/images/menubar-redesign/01-today-light.png)

![The same popover in dark](/images/menubar-redesign/01-today-dark.png)

When nothing is left the ring closes into a check.

![The popover when everything for today is done](/images/menubar-redesign/02-all-done-light.png)

![The same in dark](/images/menubar-redesign/02-all-done-dark.png)

Offline, a strip under the header says so, and the tasks you have stay.

![The popover offline, with a strip under the header](/images/menubar-redesign/03-offline-light.png)

![The same in dark](/images/menubar-redesign/03-offline-dark.png)

The ribbon unfolds what is next beside the glyph for a few seconds, then folds back.

![The menu bar unfolding what is next, on a light and a dark bar](/images/menu-bar-ribbon/01-ribbon-hold.png)

Close the day from the popover: Done, Tomorrow or No date for each task left.

![The popover closing the day](/images/menubar-redesign/04-day-close-light.png)

![The same in dark](/images/menubar-redesign/04-day-close-dark.png)

## Workspaces

An account with several workspaces opens a picker of names and project counts.

![Choose a workspace](/images/workspace-select/01-picker.png)

Switch workspaces any time in Settings.

![Settings: every workspace, the active one checked](/images/workspace-select/02-settings-workspaces.png)

## Settings

Settings groups every choice under General, Proactive, Privacy & updates, Workspaces, Workspace shortcuts and Server.

![Settings: the General and Proactive sections](/images/settings-layout/01-settings-general-dark.png)

![Settings: workspace shortcuts and the server address](/images/settings-layout/02-settings-shortcuts-server-dark.png)

![Settings in light: the General and Proactive sections](/images/settings-layout/03-settings-general-light.png)

![Settings in light: workspace shortcuts and the server address](/images/settings-layout/04-settings-shortcuts-server-light.png)

![Settings in light: the menu bar ribbon and the usage sharing switch, off by default](/images/settings-layout/05-settings-ribbon-usage-light.png)

![Settings in dark: the menu bar ribbon and the usage sharing switch, off by default](/images/settings-layout/06-settings-ribbon-usage-dark.png)

Close the day at a time you pick.

![Settings: close the day](/images/day-close/02-settings-close-the-day.png)

Turn day temperature on or off.

![Settings: the day temperature switch](/images/day-temperature/02-settings-day-temperature.png)

todz stays quiet during a Focus, and you add its filter to any Focus mode.

![Settings: the Focus row](/images/focus-aware/01-settings-focus-row.png)

![The todz Focus filter in System Settings](/images/focus-aware/02-focus-filter-sheet.png)

Get your day by email at the time you pick, and reply to add a task.

![Settings: email me my day](/images/day-email/01-settings-email-my-day.png)
