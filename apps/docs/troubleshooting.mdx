## The sign-in link does nothing, or says it could not sign you in

Open the link from the email on the same Mac that asked for it. A link opened on another Mac, or after you asked for a newer one, is refused with "Couldn't sign in with that link. Wait a minute, then open it from the email again." Ask for a new one with **Send Link** and open the newest mail.

## My agent cannot sign in with OAuth

Open the sign-in link from the mail in the same browser where you entered your email. If the client reports a redirect error, or needs a custom address scheme, todz refuses it: use a project key instead, see [Connect an agent](/connect-an-agent#with-a-key-instead).

## Too many tries

"Too many tries. Wait a minute, then try again." means the server limited the link requests. Wait a minute, then press **Send Link** once.

## macOS will not open the app

todz is not notarized, so macOS refuses it the first time. Open todz once and dismiss the warning, then go to **System Settings** › **Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** and confirm with your password or Touch ID. See [Install on Mac](/install-on-mac#1-download-and-open-it).

## Server older

**Server older** in the app means "This server is older than this app expects. Whoever runs it can update it." On your own server, deploy the current release; see [Self-hosting](/self-host).

## An update will not install

If Sparkle says todz cannot be updated from the location it was downloaded to, drag `Todz.app` into Applications, open it from there, and check again. See [Updates](/updates).
