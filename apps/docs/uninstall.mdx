---
title: Uninstall
description: Remove the app, its data and its widgets.
---

1. Quit todz, then drag `Todz.app` from Applications to the Trash. Its widgets leave the widget gallery with it.
2. Delete the app's data container, `~/Library/Containers/dev.todz.Todz`.
3. Delete the container the app shares with its widgets, `~/Library/Group Containers/XFWR794VS8.dev.todz`.

Your tasks stay on the server; only this Mac's copy is erased. To remove them too, sign in again on another Mac, or ask whoever runs your server.
