Order:

1. `server-url` merges to `main`.
2. The lead merges `origin/main` into `feat/security-fixes` (step 1), never by rebase.
3. The lead writes the tester-briefing skeleton (step 2) and branches `lane/security-fixes-w`, `lane/security-fixes-m` and `lane/security-fixes-t` from that commit, each in its own worktree.
4. The three lanes run in parallel; they share no file and write only their own section of `tester-briefing.md`. Each lane commits red first, then green, on its own branch, and never pushes. When `main` moves, the lead tells each lane what landed; a lane merges `main` at its next step boundary.
5. The lead merges the three lanes into `feat/security-fixes` by merge (step 27), runs `pnpm verify all`, then merges `origin/main` once more before `/open-pr` and again before the merge (HARNESS §7.8).

Lane M needs `xcodegen` and Xcode. Lane T's step 19 runs the macOS gate's commands on a machine with Xcode, which the developer checks with `.agents/hooks/doctor.sh` first, and runs while Lane M is idle or records the load (step 19).
