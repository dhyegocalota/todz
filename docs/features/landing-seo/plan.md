0. **Merge `main` before G0.** The lead merges `origin/main` into `feat/landing-seo` (merge, never rebase), so G0 and both lanes start from a base that holds `server-url` and every sibling already landed.
1. G0 lands first on `feat/landing-seo` (step 1): the two constant modules both lanes import, and the tester briefing's skeleton with a `## Lane H` and a `## Lane I` section, so each lane writes only its own section.
2. Lanes H and I then run in parallel, each in its own worktree on a branch from `feat/landing-seo` (`feat/landing-seo-h`, `feat/landing-seo-i`). Lane I depends on nothing from Lane H beyond G0.
3. **`security-fixes` before Lane H's shell and policy steps.** Lane H first does the steps whose files are disjoint from `security-fixes`' Lane W: the red steps 2 and 4 (new test files only; the `site-pages.test.ts` rows of step 4 go into a new `describe` block at the file's end) and steps 6 and 7 (`agent-setup.test.ts`, `agent-setup.ts`, `landing-copy.ts`). 4. **Lane I into Lane H before step 14.** When Lane I has finished step 21, the lead merges `feat/landing-seo-i` into `feat/landing-seo-h`; Lane H's step 14 (the browser checks that fetch the icons and the image) and step 16 (performance, using the WebP copies) start only after that merge.
5. The lead merges Lane H (which then holds Lane I) into `feat/landing-seo` (step 23).
6. **`docs-mintlify` before the deploy.** Step 24 (the docs page pin) and step 25 (the deploy) wait until `docs-mintlify` is on `main` and the five docs URLs answer 200 with todz's pages on production.
7. Any sibling that lands later is merged from `main` at the next step boundary; Failure modes rows M1–M4 say what collides.
